# Getting ETH to Robinhood Chain

Diesel is paid in ETH on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). This page lists only the routes in
**Robinhood Chain's own documentation**, checked on 13 October 2026. Before you bridge, open
the official page yourself and compare the address: [docs.robinhood.com/chain/bridging](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/bridging).

These are third-party services. Mohonaut has no relationship with them, is paid nothing by them
and cannot help with a transfer that went wrong.

## How much ETH you need

A game transaction costs a fraction of a cent in gas. A few dollars of ETH covers diesel for a
first site and many transactions. Start small: you can always add more.

## Routes in the chain's documentation

| Route                         | Kind                                  | Time                                       |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Arbitrum canonical bridge     | From Ethereum, trustless              | Deposit ≈ 10 min; withdrawal ≈ 7 days      |
| Relay                         | Intents-based bridge                  | Seconds                                    |
| Across                        | Intents-based bridge                  | Seconds                                    |
| LI.FI, 0x                     | Cross-chain swap aggregators          | Seconds to minutes                         |
| Stargate (LayerZero)          | Omnichain tokens                      | Minutes                                    |
| Transporter (Chainlink CCIP)  | Token transfers                       | Minutes                                    |

Follow the links on that page rather than searching for these names: search results and
ads are where fake bridges live.

### Withdrawing through the canonical bridge

Going back from Robinhood Chain to Ethereum through the Arbitrum bridge takes three steps:
start the withdrawal on Robinhood Chain, wait the 7-day challenge period, then finish it on Ethereum
(that last step costs Ethereum gas and is required).

## If your exchange or wallet supports Robinhood Chain

Some apps let you send or withdraw ETH on the Robinhood Chain network directly. If yours does,
send a small test amount first and check that it arrives in the wallet you play with.

## Stay safe

- We never send the first message, never ask for your seed phrase and never ask you to approve
  token spending. The game only asks you to pay ETH for diesel.
- A token that appears in your wallet unasked is not ours. Don't interact with it.
- There is no $MOHN market before Market Open. Anything you can buy before then is fake.
- Official addresses are only on [/contracts](/contracts/) and in the pinned post of
  @playmohonaut.
