# Refining

Mined $MOHN stays on your drill site as unrefined credit. You can spend it in the game directly,
with no fee. To move it to your wallet, refine it.

| Route           | Fee | Time          | Where the fee goes                             |
| --------------- | --- | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Standard refine | 0%  | 72-hour queue | —                                              |
| Fast refine     | 8%  | immediately   | half burned for good, half back to the reserve |
| Spend in game   | 0%  | immediately   | —                                              |

- A queued refine can be finalised by anyone once its 72 hours have passed; the game's keeper
  does it for you. The $MOHN is then minted to your wallet.
- One request refines at most 100,000 $MOHN.
- At most 150,000 $MOHN can be minted in any 60 minutes, for all players together. A refine that
  would pass that limit is refused and can be retried a little later; nothing is lost.
- Refining is closed during the 72 hours of the Genesis Dig. There is no pool before Market Open.
- There is no minimum, no KYC and no lock-up beyond the queue.

The fee and the queue length are adjustable within hard limits (0 – 12%, 24 – 168 hours), and any
change waits 96 hours in a public queue first.
