# Genesis and Market Open

The launch has three steps. Times are UTC and are announced when the contracts are deployed,
seven days before Genesis.

## Pre-fill (24 hours before Genesis)

Drill sites can be opened and tanks filled. Nothing is mined yet. During Genesis a tank holds at
most the diesel your rigs can burn until 24 hours after Market Open.

## Genesis Dig (72 hours)

Mining starts. Refining is closed, and there is no $MOHN pool: anything sold as $MOHN in these
hours is fake.

## Market Open (Genesis + 72 hours)

Anyone can call `openMarket` (the game's keeper calls it first). The contract then creates the
ETH/$MOHN pool itself, at a price computed from Genesis data, and adds the protocol's liquidity:

```
opening price = diesel bought during Genesis (USD) ÷ $MOHN mined by players during Genesis
pool $MOHN    = protocol-liquidity ETH × ETH/USD ÷ opening price      (at most 1,000,000)
```

The pool's $MOHN is minted from the reserve, within the hourly mint limit. Nobody can create the
pool earlier or at another price: the pool's hook refuses any other initialisation. The protocol's
liquidity can never be withdrawn.
