# Player guide

Mohonaut is an idle drilling game on Robinhood Chain. You run a drill site, fuel its rigs with
diesel and core one shared hole with every other player: the **World Bore**. Every $MOHN comes out
of the ground; there is no presale, no team buy and no premine.

## Your drill site

Every site starts at level 1 with a free **Pilot Rig** (10 DP, 0.25 ℓ/h). A site has **pads**
(one rig each) and a **fuel line** that limits how much diesel its rigs can burn per hour.

| Site level | Pads | Fuel line (ℓ/h) |
| ---------- | ---- | --------------- |
| 1          | 4    | 6.0             |
| 5          | 8    | 13.3            |
| 10         | 13   | 35.9            |
| 15         | 18   | 97.1            |
| 20         | 23   | 262             |

Pads grow as `3 + level`; the fuel line as `6 × 1.22^(level − 1)` ℓ/h. Levelling up costs $MOHN.

## Rigs and Drill Power

Rigs add **Drill Power (DP)** and burn diesel. Bigger rigs buy fuel efficiency (DP per ℓ/h), not
cheaper Drill Power: the price per DP rises with each tier. Rigs are bought in the **Equipment
Market**, whose prices follow a public auction formula: they rise when rigs sell faster than the
target and fall when they sell slower. Nobody sets them by hand.

You can switch rigs on and off. A rig that isn't worth its fuel can be shut down, or scrapped for
a 20% **scrap credit** that only buys new rigs and site levels.

## Diesel

Your tank is filled with ETH and measured in litres. A litre costs $0.10 at the ETH/USD price of
the Chainlink feed. Active rigs burn their litres per hour; when the tank is empty, they stop and
your Drill Power no longer counts.

ETH from diesel is routed: 55% to buyback & burn, 30% to protocol liquidity that can never be
withdrawn, 15% to operations. Published weekly on the [economy page](/economy/).

## Your share of each hour

Every hour the network cores a fixed slice of what is left in the reserve. 90% of that slice goes
to players, split by Drill Power:

```
your share of hour h = E_h × 0.90 × your active DP ÷ network active DP
```

That is all. More players means a smaller share each; nobody is promised a number. The details,
with every parameter and limit, are in the [economy paper](/docs/economy/).

## The World Bore

The bore's depth is all $MOHN mined so far ÷ 1,000, in metres. It crosses eight strata: Regolith,
Sediment Cover, Granite Basement, Archean Gneiss, Granulite Floor, Peridotite Zone, Asthenosphere
and The Core. The **Kola Line** (12,262 m) and the **Moho Line** (35,000 m) are milestones. New rig
generations unlock when the bore crosses a stratum, not on a calendar date.

Your **contribution** is your mined $MOHN ÷ 1,000 in metres: "You've cored 1,240 m of the World Bore".

## The Refinery

Mined $MOHN can be spent in the game directly. To take it to your wallet, refine it: a free
standard refine waits 72 hours, a fast refine costs 8% (half burned, half back to the reserve).
See [Refining](/docs/refining/).

## Crews, Seasons and the Survey

- **Crews** gather 5 to 25 players. They are social and cosmetic; emission is never split by crew.
- **Seasons** last about eight weeks and rank World Bore contribution, fuel efficiency and crew
  play. Rewards are badges and titles.
- The **Survey** is a free daily find for your Core Collection: a core sample, a fossil or a piece
  of drilling history. It has no value in the economy, and nothing in Mohonaut is a paid roll.

## Invites

You can invite one level of players. Once someone you invited has bought at least 0.003 ETH of
diesel, and while your own rigs are fuelled, you receive 3% of their mined share. It is paid from
the treasury's 5%: never taken from them, and no new emission is created.
