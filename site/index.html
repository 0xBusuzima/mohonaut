MOHONAUT

Not the moon. The Moho.

The deepest hole humans ever drilled stopped at 12,262 m. The Moho, the boundary between Earth's crust and mantle, sits about 35,000 m down.

Mohonaut is an idle drilling game where every player drills the same borehole. Run a night-lit drill site, keep your rigs fuelled, and push the World Bore deeper, together.

  • One shared borehole. Every metre comes from players' rigs.
  • No presale. No team buy. Every token comes out of the ground.
  • Rules published before launch and enforced by verified contracts.
In development. There is no token, no contract and no sale today. Anyone offering MOHN right now is not us. We never DM first and never ask for funds or seed phrases.
